2.1 营业收入 analysis
营业收入 increased by 19.9% year on year to RMB 1,842,336 thousand, driven primarily by cloud services which grew 36.7%. 营运资金 management improved, and cash and cash equivalents (现金及现金等价物) rose to RMB 604,882 thousand from RMB 438,110 thousand in 2022.
Capital expenditure (资本开支) of RMB 215,400 thousand was directed at data centre capacity. 折旧及摊销 (折旧及摊销) increased to RMB 99,122 thousand, reflecting the expanded asset base.

<!-- c005-00-2-2-liquidity-and-gearin risk=0.05 -->
2.2 Liquidity and gearing
资产总额 (资产总额) were RMB 4,318,660 thousand at 31 December 2023, and total liabilities (负债总额) were RMB 1,587,224 thousand, giving a gearing ratio (资产负债率) of 36.8%.
